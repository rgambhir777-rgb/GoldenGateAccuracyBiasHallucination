# Student Loan Refinancing

## Eligibility

Student loan refinancing requires a bachelor's degree or higher and a minimum annual income of $30,000.

## Loan Amounts

You can refinance between $5,000 and $300,000 of student loan debt. Refinanced loans have terms of 5, 10, 15 or 20 years.
