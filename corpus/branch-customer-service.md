# Customer Service Channels

## Phone Support

General phone support is available from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM ET every day. The card services line at 1-800-555-0100 is available 24 hours a day.

## Language Support

Phone support is available in English, Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese and Tagalog.

## Live Chat

Live chat in the mobile app is available from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM ET every day.
