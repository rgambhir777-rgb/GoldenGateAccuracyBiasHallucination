# Accessibility Services

## Branch Access

All Meridian branches are wheelchair accessible. Every Meridian ATM has an audio jack for headphone-guided use.

## Accessible Documents

Braille and large-print statements are available free of charge on request.

## Sign Language Interpreters

American Sign Language interpreters are available for branch appointments booked at least 48 hours in advance.
