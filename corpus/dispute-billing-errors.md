# Credit Card Billing Errors

## Notice Requirements

Credit card billing errors must be reported in writing within 60 days of the statement date.

## Investigation

Meridian acknowledges a billing error notice within 30 days. The investigation is completed within 2 billing cycles and no later than 90 days.
