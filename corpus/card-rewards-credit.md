# Meridian Rewards Visa

## Cash Back

The Meridian Rewards Visa earns 1.5% cash back on all purchases. Grocery purchases earn 3% cash back on up to $6,000 of spending per year.

## Annual Fee and APR

The Meridian Rewards Visa has no annual fee. The purchase APR is a variable 20.24% to 28.24%.
