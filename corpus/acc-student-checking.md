# Student Checking Account

## Eligibility

Student Checking is available to full-time students aged 17 to 24 with a valid student ID. A parent or guardian must co-own the account for students under 18.

## Fees and ATM Access

Student Checking has no monthly maintenance fee. Students receive 4 free out-of-network ATM withdrawals per month.

## Conversion After Graduation

When the account holder turns 25, the account converts automatically to Everyday Checking. Customers are notified 30 days before the conversion.
