# Official Checks and Stop Payments

## Cashier's Checks

A cashier's check costs $10 and a money order costs $5. Premier Checking customers receive cashier's checks free of charge.

## Stop Payment Orders

A stop payment order costs $30 per request. A stop payment order stays in effect for 6 months and can be renewed.
