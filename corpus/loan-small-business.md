# Small Business Loans

## Eligibility

Small business loan applicants must have been operating for at least 2 years with annual revenue of $100,000 or more.

## Products

Meridian offers business term loans from $10,000 to $500,000 and business lines of credit up to $250,000.

## Required Documents

Applicants must provide 2 years of business tax returns and 3 months of bank statements.
