# Returned Items and Research Fees

## Returned Deposited Items

If a check you deposit is returned unpaid, Meridian charges a returned deposited item fee of $15.

## Account Research

Account research or statement reconstruction beyond the first hour is billed at $25 per hour.

## Expedited Card Delivery

Standard delivery of a replacement debit card is free. Expedited delivery of a replacement debit card costs $20 and arrives in 2 business days.
