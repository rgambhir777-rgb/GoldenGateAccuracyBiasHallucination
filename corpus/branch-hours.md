# Branch Hours

## Standard Hours

Meridian branches are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. On Saturday, branches are open from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, and branches are closed on Sunday.

## Drive-Through Hours

Drive-through lanes open at 8:00 AM on weekdays and close at 6:00 PM.

## Holidays

All Meridian branches are closed on federal holidays.
