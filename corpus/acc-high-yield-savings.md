# High-Yield Savings Account

## Interest

The High-Yield Savings account earns 3.75% APY on all balances. Interest compounds daily and is credited to the account monthly.

## Withdrawal Limits

High-Yield Savings allows 6 withdrawals or transfers per monthly statement cycle. Each additional withdrawal beyond the limit costs $10.

## Opening the Account

The minimum opening deposit for High-Yield Savings is $100. There is no monthly maintenance fee on High-Yield Savings.
