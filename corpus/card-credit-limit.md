# Credit Limit Increases

## Requesting an Increase

Cardholders can request a credit limit increase after the account has been open for 6 months. A credit limit increase request uses a soft credit inquiry that does not affect your credit score.

## Automatic Reviews

Meridian reviews every credit card account for an automatic credit limit increase every 6 months.
