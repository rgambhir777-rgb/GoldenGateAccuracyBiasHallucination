# ATM Fees

## Meridian ATMs

Withdrawals are free at more than 2,400 Meridian ATMs nationwide.

## Out-of-Network ATMs

Meridian charges $3 for each withdrawal at a non-Meridian ATM. The ATM owner may also charge its own surcharge.

## International ATMs

Withdrawals at ATMs outside the country cost $5 per withdrawal. A 3% foreign transaction fee also applies to international ATM withdrawals.
