# Premier Checking Account

## Eligibility

Premier Checking is designed for customers who hold combined balances of $25,000 or more across Meridian deposit and investment accounts.

## Monthly Fee

Premier Checking has a monthly fee of $30. The fee is waived when combined balances stay at $25,000 or more.

## Premier Benefits

Premier customers get a dedicated relationship manager and unlimited ATM fee refunds worldwide. Premier customers also have access to a priority phone line that is staffed 24 hours a day.
