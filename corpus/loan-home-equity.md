# Home Equity Line of Credit

## Borrowing Limit

A home equity line of credit lets you borrow up to 85% of your home's value minus your mortgage balance.

## Draw and Repayment

The home equity line of credit has a 10-year draw period followed by a 20-year repayment period.

## Fees

There are no closing costs for home equity lines up to $250,000. The home equity line has a $99 annual fee that is waived in the first year.
