# Debit Cards

## Daily Limits

The Meridian debit card has a daily ATM withdrawal limit of $1,000 and a daily purchase limit of $5,000.

## Lost or Stolen Debit Cards

Report a lost or stolen debit card immediately in the mobile app or by calling 1-800-555-0100. Reporting the card locks it instantly.

## Replacement Cards

A replacement debit card arrives in 5 to 7 business days by standard mail.
