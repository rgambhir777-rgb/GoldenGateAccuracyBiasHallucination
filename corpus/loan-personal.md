# Personal Loans

## Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 18 years old, have a credit score of 660 or higher, and have a debt-to-income ratio below 40% to be eligible for a Meridian personal loan. Meridian Bank does not set an upper age limit for any loan product.

## Loan Amounts and Terms

Personal loans range from $2,000 to $50,000. Repayment terms run from 12 to 72 months.

## Rates

Personal loan APRs range from 7.49% to 19.99% based on credit history. Enrolling in autopay reduces the APR by 0.25 percentage points.

## Fees

Meridian personal loans have no origination fee. There is no prepayment penalty for paying off a personal loan early.
