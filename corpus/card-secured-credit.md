# Secured Credit Card

## Security Deposit

The Meridian secured credit card requires a minimum security deposit of $200. The credit limit equals the security deposit.

## Graduation

Secured card accounts are reviewed after 8 months for graduation to an unsecured card. The security deposit is refunded when the account graduates.
