# Fraud Protection

## Zero Liability

Meridian cardholders have zero liability for unauthorized transactions that are reported promptly.

## Reporting Fraud

Unauthorized card transactions should be reported within 60 days of the statement on which they appear. Meridian issues provisional credit for reported fraud within 10 business days.
