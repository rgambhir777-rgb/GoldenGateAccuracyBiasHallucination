# Mortgages

## Programs

Meridian offers 30-year fixed, 15-year fixed and 7/1 adjustable-rate mortgages.

## Down Payment

First-time homebuyers can put down as little as 3%. Other buyers need a down payment of at least 5%, and private mortgage insurance is required when the down payment is below 20%.

## Preapproval

A Meridian mortgage preapproval is valid for 90 days. Preapproval requires 2 years of income documentation.
