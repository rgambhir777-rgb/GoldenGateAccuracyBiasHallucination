# Check Fraud and Forgery

## Reporting a Forged Check

Report a forged or altered check within 30 days of the statement date. You must complete an affidavit of forgery at a branch.

## Investigation

Meridian completes check fraud investigations within 10 business days of receiving the affidavit.
