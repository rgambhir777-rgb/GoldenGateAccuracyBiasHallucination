# Overdraft Fees and Protection

## Overdraft Fee

The overdraft fee is $35 per item paid into overdraft. Meridian charges a maximum of 3 overdraft fees per business day.

## Overdraft Protection

Linking a Meridian savings account provides overdraft protection. Each overdraft protection transfer costs $10.

## Overdraft Grace

No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $50 or less at the end of the business day. If you bring the balance positive by 11:59 PM ET on the next business day, the overdraft fee is refunded.
