# Credit Card Payments

## Minimum Payment

The minimum payment is the greater of $35 or 1% of the balance plus interest and fees.

## Late Fees and Grace Period

A late payment fee of up to $40 applies when the minimum payment is not received by the due date. Meridian credit cards have a grace period of 25 days on new purchases when the balance is paid in full.
