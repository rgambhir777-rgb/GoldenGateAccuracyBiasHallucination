# Coin and Cash Services

## Coin Counting

Coin counting is free for Meridian customers at branches with coin machines. Non-customers pay 8% of the counted amount.

## Large Cash Withdrawals

Cash withdrawals over $10,000 at a branch require 2 business days of advance notice.
