# Meridian Pay Person-to-Person Transfers

## Limits

Meridian Pay person-to-person transfers are limited to $2,500 per day and $10,000 per 30 days.

## Speed and Cost

Meridian Pay transfers are free and arrive in minutes when the recipient is enrolled.

## Cancellations

A Meridian Pay transfer cannot be canceled once the recipient is enrolled. Only send money to people you know and trust.
