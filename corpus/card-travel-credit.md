# Meridian Voyager Card

## Travel Rewards

The Meridian Voyager card earns 2 miles per dollar on travel purchases and 1 mile per dollar on everything else.

## Fees

The Meridian Voyager card has an annual fee of $95. The Voyager card charges no foreign transaction fees.
