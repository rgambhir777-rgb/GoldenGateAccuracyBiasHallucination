# Unauthorized ACH Debits

## Reporting

Unauthorized ACH debits must be reported within 60 days of the statement date. You will be asked to sign a written statement of unauthorized debit.

## Refunds

Once the written statement is received, Meridian refunds an unauthorized ACH debit within 5 business days.
