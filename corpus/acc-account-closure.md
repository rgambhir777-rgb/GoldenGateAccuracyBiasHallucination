# Closing an Account

## How to Close

You can close a Meridian account online, by phone or at any branch. Accounts closed within 90 days of opening are charged a $25 early closure fee.

## Final Balance

The remaining balance of a closed account is mailed by check within 10 business days or transferred to another Meridian account.

## Dormant Accounts

Accounts with no customer activity for 24 months are classified as dormant. Dormant balances are reported to the state as unclaimed property after 36 months of inactivity.
