# Using Cards Abroad

## Foreign Transaction Fees

Meridian debit cards and the Rewards Visa charge a 3% foreign transaction fee on purchases made outside the country.

## Travel Notifications

You do not need to file a travel notice before going abroad. Meridian's fraud monitoring uses card activity and device location to recognize travel.
