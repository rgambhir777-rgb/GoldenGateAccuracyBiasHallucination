# Everyday Checking Account

## Overview

Everyday Checking is Meridian Bank's standard checking account for daily spending. It includes a contactless debit card, online banking and mobile check deposit.

## Monthly Maintenance Fee

The monthly maintenance fee for Everyday Checking is $12. The fee is waived when you keep a minimum daily balance of $1,500 or receive direct deposits totaling $500 or more per month.

## Opening Requirements

You need a minimum opening deposit of $25 to open an Everyday Checking account. Applicants must be at least 18 years old and provide a government-issued photo ID.
