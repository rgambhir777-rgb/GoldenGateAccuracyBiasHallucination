# Auto Loans

## Eligibility

Auto loan applicants need a credit score of 620 or higher to be eligible. The vehicle must be no older than 10 years and have fewer than 125,000 miles.

## Terms

New vehicle loans have terms of up to 84 months. Used vehicle loans have terms of up to 72 months.

## Down Payment

Meridian finances up to 100% of the purchase price of a new vehicle for applicants with a credit score above 720. Other applicants need a down payment of at least 10%.
