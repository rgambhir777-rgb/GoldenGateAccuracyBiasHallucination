# Joint Accounts

## Ownership

Meridian joint accounts can have up to 4 owners. Each joint owner has equal access to all funds in the account.

## Removing an Owner

Removing an owner from a joint account requires the written consent of all owners at a branch.

## Survivorship

When a joint owner dies, the funds pass to the surviving owners by right of survivorship.
