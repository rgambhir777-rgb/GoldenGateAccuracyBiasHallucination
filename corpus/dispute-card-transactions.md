# Disputing a Card Transaction

## How to Dispute

To dispute a card transaction, open the transaction in the mobile app and select Dispute, or call 1-800-555-0100.

## Dispute Deadline

You must dispute a card transaction within 60 days of the statement date on which the charge first appeared.

## Dispute Timeline

Meridian resolves most card disputes within 45 days. For debit card disputes, provisional credit is issued within 10 business days.
