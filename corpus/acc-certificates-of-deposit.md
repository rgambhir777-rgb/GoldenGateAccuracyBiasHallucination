# Certificates of Deposit

## Terms and Minimums

Meridian certificates of deposit are offered in 6, 12, 24 and 60 month terms. Every certificate of deposit requires a minimum deposit of $1,000.

## Rates

The 12-month certificate of deposit earns 4.10% APY. The 60-month certificate of deposit earns 3.60% APY.

## Early Withdrawal Penalty

The early withdrawal penalty is 90 days of interest for certificate terms of 12 months or less. For certificate terms longer than 12 months the penalty is 180 days of interest.

## Maturity and Grace Period

Certificates renew automatically at maturity for the same term. A 10-day grace period after maturity lets you withdraw funds or change the term without penalty.
