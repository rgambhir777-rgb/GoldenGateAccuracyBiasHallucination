# Financial Hardship Assistance

## Payment Deferral

Customers facing financial hardship can request a payment deferral of up to 3 months in any 12-month period. Interest continues to accrue during a deferral.

## Contacting the Hardship Team

The hardship team can be reached at 1-800-555-0144 from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM ET on weekdays. Requesting assistance does not affect your credit score.
