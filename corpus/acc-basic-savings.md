# Basic Savings Account

## Overview

Basic Savings earns 0.50% APY and is a simple way to start saving. Basic Savings has a $5 monthly fee that is waived with a balance of $300 or a linked Meridian checking account.

## Savings for Minors

Customers under 18 can open Basic Savings with a parent or guardian as joint owner. Accounts for minors have no monthly fee.
