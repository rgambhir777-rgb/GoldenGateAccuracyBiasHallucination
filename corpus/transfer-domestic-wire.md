# Domestic Wire Transfers

## Fees

An outgoing domestic wire transfer costs $25. An incoming domestic wire transfer costs $15. Premier Checking customers pay no fees for domestic wire transfers.

## Cutoff

Domestic wire requests received before the 5:00 PM ET cutoff are delivered the same business day.
